How CivicResolve Operates

From citizen snap to on-ground municipal action, our platform removes friction at every step.

Step 1

1. Citizen Reports

Citizens submit photo, description, and GPS coordinates via browser in under 30 seconds. No login required.

Step 2

2. AI Auto-Triage

Gemini (or offline Demo AI) analyzes text and imagery to classify category, urgency (Critical to Low), and safety risk.

Step 3

3. Smart Routing

Automatically assigned to Roads, Sanitation, Electricity, Water, or Drainage departments with a unique tracking ID.

Step 4

4. Public Tracking

Citizens track real-time status changes and official staff remarks transparently on the public timeline.

AI Urgency Assessment

How Our AI Prioritizes Civic Hazards

Our model assesses the immediate threat to life, public health, and urban mobility to sort complaints into 4 strict tiers.

Critical Priority < 4 hr response

Life Threat & Emergencies

Open manholes near schools, high-pressure water main bursts, exposed sparking live wires, collapsed road sections.

High Priority < 24 hr response

Major Arterial Blockers

Deep crater potholes on highways/bus stands, heavy construction rubble dumped on road shoulders, asphalt cave-ins.

Medium Priority < 48 hr response

Sanitation & Outages

Overflowing commercial dumpsters, multi-lamp street blackout, silted drains creating foul stagnant pools.

Low Priority Scheduled

Routine & Aesthetic

Single flickering bulb on jogging path, minor slow drip at municipal public drinking fountain.
